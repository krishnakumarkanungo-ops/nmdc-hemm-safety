THE PHYSICS OF ZERO-VISIBILITY: WHY OPTICAL & RF SYSTEMS INEVITABLY FAIL

Optical cameras (Caterpillar) scatter light completely when water droplets in monsoon fog or airborne hematite dust particles reach 2–10 microns. RF-only tags (Hexagon) can only detect machines equipped with costly battery-powered transponders. If an un-tagged light vehicle, stray cow, fallen boulder, or un-tagged contractor walks into the pit, Hexagon is 100% blind. Our system combines 77 GHz mmWave FMCW radar (which transmits wavelengths that pass unimpeded through fog and dust) with Long-Wave Infrared (LWIR) thermal optics. We guarantee 100m physical obstacle detection under zero-lux pitch darkness and 100% code-black fog.

2

ACTIVE LEVEL 9 INTERVENTION VS "FATAL ALARM FATIGUE"

In typical open-cast operations, drivers receive over 300 false or nuisance audio beeps per shift from Hexagon or Cat systems. This triggers severe Alarm Fatigue: drivers either learn to subconsciously ignore the warning or physically tape over the in-cab buzzer. In contrast, our prototype adheres to ISO 21815-2 Level 9 standard: we use an autonomous hardware fail-safe (ESP32) that computes real-time Time-To-Collision (TTC). If the driver fails to react within the safe stopping window, our controller directly de-energizes the throttle PWM and fires the hydraulic retarder brake solenoid to physically halt the 100-ton truck before impact.

3

TACKLING DGMS FATAL ACCIDENT CAUSE #1: BERM DROP-OFFS & ROLLOVERS

According to the Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS), over 42% of fatal heavy earth-moving machinery (HEMM) accidents occur due to dumper reversal over bench berms and ramp rollovers on sharp switchbacks. None of the four global giants provide dedicated side-clearance laser measurement. Our prototype mounts dual VL53L1X Time-of-Flight (ToF) Class-1 Lasers measuring the exact distance to the safety berm in real-time, coupled with an MPU6050 6-Axis Digital Inclinometer tracking road cant and ramp pitch. If the machine drifts within 1.5m of the bench cliff edge or exceeds safe bank grade, the system instantly flashes an emergency corrective advisory.

4

100% OEM-AGNOSTIC RETROFITTING & ZERO VENDOR LOCK-IN

Mining companies in India (NMDC, Coal India, Tata Steel, Vedanta) operate heterogeneous mixed fleets: Caterpillar dumpers, Komatsu shovels, BEML rear-dumpers, Volvo tippers, and Tata water tankers. Installing Cat MineStar on a BEML truck is virtually impossible without paying exorbitant OEM custom engineering fees. Our system is built on open industrial protocols (CAN J1939, RS485, Web Serial, and RESTful WebSocket architecture). Any rugged Android tablet, iPad, or vehicle terminal running a modern browser can serve as the in-cab display with zero client software installation.

5

DECENTRALIZED V2V MESH: 100% INDEPENDENT OF CELLULAR / INTERNET TOWERS

Open-pit mines like NMDC Bailadila Deposit 14 descend several hundred meters into deep geological gorges where 4G/5G cellular towers and line-of-sight Wi-Fi access points suffer massive multipath interference and blackouts. If the central tower fails, cloud-dependent dispatch systems disconnect. Our system integrates autonomous Peer-to-Peer (P2P) V2V mesh (ESP-NOW / Sub-GHz LoRa). Trucks continuously broadcast speed, heading, and brake telemetry directly to nearby vehicles within a 500m radius with zero cellular network or central server dependency.
